Hello, fellow wanderer. I'm Bryan, an urban planner based in Oregon. I enjoy writing, photography, and travel. Low Velocity is where I write at length about cities and share photographs from being out in the world.

My work explores the spaces we share and the patterns that give them life. I photograph cities, civic places, and landscapes in search of beauty and meaning. I write to learn, to grow, and to make sense of both myself and the world.

I'm drawn to how the built and natural environments shape the way we live. I believe design profoundly influences our emotions, wellbeing, and health. My work sits at the intersection of design, environmental psychology, and human geography.

Follow along by RSS or JSON Feed, on Bluesky at @lowvelocity.org, or in any standard.site reader. You can also just say hello. There's a newsletter too, but I almost never send it.

Shorter posts, check-ins and the everyday stuff live at bryanrobb.com.

Colophon

This site is a constant work in progress because I am a tinkerer, not a programmer or web designer by training.

It runs on Ghost, with a custom theme built from Ghost's Dawn and inspired by Pika.page and Astro Cactus. A small Cloudflare Worker connects it to the AT Protocol: each new essay is published to standard.site and shared from the Low Velocity Bluesky account, and replies there show up under the post.

If something looks a little broken, it probably is. I am likely already fiddling with it.
